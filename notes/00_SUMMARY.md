# 00 — PROJECT SUMMARY

**Project:** The Abandoned Horizon
**Universe:** Digital Decay
**Genre:** Liminal Space / Siege / Survival Horror
**Setting:** A four-acre brutalist school complex on the Alaskan coast
**Structure:** 24 chapters, 4 acts

---

## LOGLINE
Twenty years after graduation, five old friends return to their Alaskan high school for a class reunion—just as a supernatural blizzard seals the town inside, and the school begins to come apart at the seams.

---

## CORE PREMISE
- The friends each receive a **letter in the mail** for the **20th-anniversary reunion** of their graduating class—sent by **Alex** to the *entire* class. Only five came.
- They **meet back up at the airport in Alaska** and travel together to the school.
- The school is a **real, functional school** that also serves as the town's **designated storm shelter**. In a bad blow, the whole town retreats into the massive **sub-basement**.
- A **"White-Out" blizzard** traps them. It is the Void pressing against the town.
- The school begins to **lose cohesion**. Reality "melts" into a liminal, low-resolution state.
- **Alex**, the Custodian, is caught in-between dimensions, trying to "re-boot" the school—but every re-boot accelerates the decay.
- Their memory of high school is **blurry**—warm, but full of holes. Two causes: they **suppressed** parts, and **the place makes anyone who leaves forget**. What's left is **jumbled fragments**—snippets out of order.

---

## THE ENDING (LOCKED)
- **One ending only.** They **escape**, and the world is **re-stabilized** afterward.
- The old "Cycle Restarts" ending is **cut**.
- **But the story contains loops** (the re-boot cycles)—~10–12 of them, each a little different, each harder to reset until the second-to-last. See `01_WORLD_LORE.md`.
- **Time inside:** it feels like ~3 months to them; **4.5 years** pass outside.
- **Archive:** they find **five dossiers** (their own) + the **roster**—personal details, physical descriptions, and a **Retention** score (% compatibility with the Void / the Noise). The five sit at **73–88%**; **Alex is 100%**.
- **The school:** **~12 buildings, 10–12 stories each**, packed into ~4 acres—a **vertical greenhouse**, a **separate enormous Library**, academic blocks, dormitories, kitchen / staff / living buildings—fused by corridors because the storms never stop.
- **Loop structure:** **Ch 1 = the first loop (full)**; the middle loops are **compressed**; the **last couple chapters = the final loop (full)**.
- **Carries over:** digital (fully), key items (key cards, keys, photos, notes), memories (partially). **Critical injuries carry but fade each loop; minor damage resets.**
- **Alex:** grows **more desperate** with every loop.
