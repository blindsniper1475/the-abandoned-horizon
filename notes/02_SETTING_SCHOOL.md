# 02 — SETTING: THE SCHOOL

---

## LOCATION
A sprawling, **brutalist compound of interconnected buildings** on the rugged, fog-heavy coast of Alaska.
Roughly **four acres** in footprint.

## THE COMPOUND (12 BUILDINGS)
It is not one building. It is a **campus of about twelve tall buildings**, stitched together—**ten to twelve stories each**, packed into the four-acre footprint and joined by **enclosed corridors, tunnels, and covered walkways**.
- **The Library** — its own **separate, enormous building**. The tallest, quietest structure on the campus.
- **The Greenhouse** — a **vertical greenhouse**, floor stacked on floor of glass and growing light.
- **The Academic Blocks** — classrooms.
- **The Dormitories** — living quarters.
- **The Service Blocks** — kitchen, staff, and residential space.
- They are interconnected **because the storms never stop.** In that country you cannot walk between buildings; everything is fused so the town can move without ever going outside.
- The **sub-basement** runs beneath all of it, linking every building below ground.

## FUNCTION
It is a **real, functional school**—but it was also designed to double as the **town's emergency shelter**.
- When the weather turns lethal (the "White-Out"), the townsfolk retreat inside.
- The school is built to hold an entire population.

## THE SUB-BASEMENT
A **massive subterranean level** spanning the full four-acre footprint and linking every building above.
- Houses the **dorms / living quarters**.
- Serves as the **storm shelter** for the whole town.
- Contains the "Deep Archive."

## THE SIEGE (THE BLIZZARD)
A supernatural blizzard—the **"White-Out"**—acts as a physical cage.
It is "White Noise": it erases the horizon and the town, and prevents escape.

## THE ECHOES (THE MONSTERS)
Entities born from the Rot. They are the "discarded" pieces of reality trying to break into the school's safe zone.
Three kinds (full detail in `01_WORLD_LORE.md`):
- **Static Echoes** — frozen, looping, harmless alone but endless.
- **Corrupted Echoes** — aggressive, hunting distortions of discarded people.
- **Buffer-Breakers** — huge, non-Euclidean; they warp geometry and erode the school.

## THE BUILDINGS
About **twelve buildings, ten to twelve stories each**, packed into roughly four acres and fused by enclosed corridors.
- **The Library** — its own separate, enormous building.
- **The Greenhouse** — a vertical greenhouse, stacked floor on floor.
- Plus academic blocks, dormitories, and kitchen / staff / living buildings.
*(Full detail in `02_SETTING_SCHOOL.md`.)*
