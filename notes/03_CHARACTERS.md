# 03 — CHARACTERS OVERVIEW

*Five old friends. Twenty years of adult life sits on top of blurry high-school memories.*

Full one-page profiles live in `characters/` (one file per character).

---

## MARCUS — The Protector
- **Life:** Texas Ranger. Married to Elena. Lives in Texas.
- **Look:** Tall, broad-shouldered, weathered. Short hair, a slight beard, calloused hands.
- **Personality:** Stoic, steady, the anchor of the group. A weary weight behind the eyes.
- **Goal:** Keep everyone standing.
- **Under decay:** Fixates on physical barriers; becomes aggressive holding the safe zone.

## ELENA — The Record Keeper
- **Life:** Online reporter. Married to Marcus. Lives in Texas.
- **Look:** Sharp-eyed, practical, polished. Hair pulled back; dressed for movement.
- **Personality:** Meticulous, curious, quietly anxious when things don't add up.
- **Goal:** Find the truth of their past.
- **Under decay:** Obsessed with the glitches; reads them like a language.

## SARAH — The Observer
- **Life:** Model / funder. Married to David. Lives in Oregon.
- **Look:** Tall, poised, camera-ready. Graceful stillness.
- **Personality:** Quiet, introspective, watches more than she speaks.
- **Goal:** Hold onto her sense of self.
- **Under decay:** Most sensitive; withdraws as she feels herself lose definition.

## DAVID — The Analyst
- **Life:** Systems / security analyst. Married to Sarah. Lives in Oregon.
- **Look:** Lean, slightly stooped, restless hands, tired sharp eyes.
- **Personality:** Quick, logical, over-analyzes; frustrated by what he can't fix.
- **Goal:** Find the backdoor out.
- **Under decay:** Obsessed with measuring the decay.

## JAKE — The Witness
- **Life:** Hippie photographer. Single, drifting, no fixed home.
- **Look:** Lanky, long unkempt hair, bohemian clothes, beaded bracelet.
- **Personality:** Easy-going, empathetic, the "soul" of the group.
- **Goal:** Remember the lost.
- **Under decay:** Feels sorrow rather than fear for the things outside.

## THE PAIRINGS
- **Marcus & Elena** — married, Texas. The anchor couple.
- **Sarah & David** — married, Oregon. The couple who watch and measure.
- **Jake** — alone, drifting. The witness.

## THE REUNION
They were classmates. Twenty years later, **Alex** mails a **20th-anniversary reunion letter** to the *entire* graduating class.
Only these **five** show up. The rest never came.
They meet at the **airport in Alaska**. This is the first time they've all been in a room together in two decades.

## THE BLURRY YEARS
None of them can clearly recall high school, for two compounding reasons (see `07_THE_LETTER_AND_MEMORY.md`):
- **Suppression** — they each buried parts of it themselves.
- **The Place** — anyone who leaves the school forgetting what happened there.
What survives are **fragments**—snippets, jumbled and out of order.

---

## UNDER DECAY — THE DURESS SIGNATURES
As the school comes apart, so do they. Everyone follows the same arc—**flu, then disorientation, then fractured memory, then forgetting**—but each person *shows* it differently.

- **Marcus** — denies it longest. Treats the "chill" as a cold he can walk off. His body holds while his judgment erodes; he turns short-tempered, then paranoid about the walls. The last to admit anything is wrong.
- **Elena** — notices first. Her tell is that her own notes stop matching her memory—entries in her own handwriting she doesn't remember making.
- **Sarah** — loses visual resolution first. Colors dull, edges soften, her reflection lags a half-second behind her. She feels herself going *flat*.
- **David** — his logic turns on him. He runs the same calculation twice and gets two answers. Migraines, ringing ears, and a growing distrust of his own mind.
- **Jake** — the most open. He starts remembering things that never happened to him; his camera catches people who aren't there. He grieves for the Echoes as though they were friends.
