# 04 — THE 24-CHAPTER ROADMAP

*A slow-burn progression of decay and siege. Buffer: 100% → 0%.*

> **The loops:** the siege repeats (**~10–12 loops**, each a little different). Resets get harder until the **second-to-last**, when remembering past loops finally makes it easier. The final loop is the escape. See `01_WORLD_LORE.md → THE LOOPS`.
>
> **How the loops are shown:**
> - **Ch 1 = the first loop, in full.**
> - **The middle loops are compressed**—montaged, echoed, glimpsed.
> - **The last couple of chapters = the final loop, in full.**

---

## ACT I: THE PRISTINE FACADE (Ch 1–6)
**Theme:** Nostalgia, isolation, and the too-perfect surface.
- **Ch 1: The Arrival** — Arrival at the school during the first blizzard. Everything is pristine.
- **Ch 2: The Sanctuary** — Settling in. Eerie, but peaceful.
- **Ch 3: The Archive Vaults** — First discovery of the discarded records.
- **Ch 4: The First Echo** — A corrupted Echo manifests during the blizzard.
- **Ch 5: The Hallway Stretch** — First experience of non-Euclidean geometry.
- **Ch 6: The Gatekeepers** — They find the records of the Custodian (Alex).

## ACT II: THE EROSION (Ch 7–12)
**Theme:** The De-Rez begins. The Buffer starts to crack.
- **Ch 7: The First Crack** — First phosphor-green glitches appear.
- **Ch 8: The Pool** — They see the decay of the town in the water.
- **Ch 9: The Siege Intensifies** — The Echoes grow aggressive; the storm grows louder.
- **Ch 10: The Greenhouse** — The Rot is strongest here.
- **Ch 11: The Memory Fracture** — The hard-coded memories start to fail.
- **Ch 12: The Clock Tower** — They find the Custodian's logs and learn about the re-boot.

## ACT III: THE FRACTURE (Ch 13–18)
**Theme:** The Buffer fails. The school becomes a labyrinth.
- **Ch 13: The Failure** — Buffer hits 50%. The school begins to melt.
- **Ch 14: The Echo Chamber** — The Echoes enter the school. The siege becomes survival.
- **Ch 15: The Non-Euclidean Maze** — Geometry becomes impossible to navigate.
- **Ch 16: The Archive's Shadow** — A hidden archive: the secrets of the discarded subjects.
- **Ch 17: The Void Signal** — The White-Out reaches its peak.
- **Ch 18: The Gate Collapse** — They witness the De-Rez of the town's perimeter.

## ACT IV: THE FINAL DE-REZ (Ch 19–24)
**Theme:** The collapse—and the escape.
- **Ch 19: The Pool's Depths** — Buffer hits 10%. They enter the pool for the final sink.
- **Ch 20: The Final De-Rez** — Resolution hits zero. Low-Bit geometry takes over.
- **Ch 21: The Loss** — Jake is deleted as the Buffer fails.
- **Ch 22: The Void Floods In** — The school is no longer a school.
- **Ch 23: The Escape** — Only one way out: through. They commit to the escape.
- **Ch 24: Re-Stabilization** — They get out. The world comes back, re-stabilized.

> **ENDING (LOCKED):** One ending only. They **escape**, and everything is **re-stabilized** afterward—the book does not end on a branch. The old "Cycle Restarts" ending is cut. **But the story contains loops** (see the note at the top of this file).
