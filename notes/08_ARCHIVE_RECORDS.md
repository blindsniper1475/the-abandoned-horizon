# 08 — THE ARCHIVE RECORDS (FOUND DOCUMENTS)

*What the friends uncover in Act II. In-scene these are physical files, brittle printouts, and dead screens. The numbers here are internal reference.*

---

## WHAT THEY ARE
Every person ever processed through the school has a **dossier**. Strip away the institutional language and each one holds the same three things:
1. **Personal details** — who they were.
2. **Physical description** — what they looked like.
3. **RETENTION** — their **compatibility with the Void / the Noise** (%).

In Act II the friends find **five dossiers**—*their own*—and the **roster** that indexes everyone.

---

## WHY THE RECORDS MATTER
- Their own pasts are in the files—**personal details they don't remember living.**
- The **retention numbers** explain *why them*: not love of the school, not luck. **Compatibility.**
- And they explain **Alex**: 100%—bound forever to the place he can never leave.
- The five are **not special**. Higher scorers existed. They just didn't come.
