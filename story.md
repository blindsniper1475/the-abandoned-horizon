# The Abandoned Horizon

## Synopsis

Twenty years after graduation, five classmates answer a reunion letter and drive into an Alaskan blizzard, back to the school that was built to shelter a whole town. The town is gone. The school is pristine — and then it begins to lose resolution. The same season repeats, again and again, while the man who mailed the letters tries, one re-boot at a time, to save them.

## Tone & Style

Grounded and human before it is strange: small-town dread in the Stephen King register — marriages, old jobs, a body that is tired — with the uncanny folded in quietly underneath. The decay is never explained. It is felt as *flatness*, *wrongness*, *washed-out light*, a hallway that took four steps longer than it did the day before.
