# Why Did Alex Send The Letter

## Question

Alex mailed a twentieth-anniversary reunion invitation to the entire graduating class. It was not a reunion. Why did he send it, and why did only five people come?

## Evidence

- Every surviving classmate received a letter; only Marcus, Elena, Sarah, David, and Jake answered.
- None of the five can explain why they came — only that they felt they had to.
- Once the White-Out closes, the school cannot be reached from outside.
- Nothing in the letters mentions the storms, the school closing, or Alex himself.
- Alex is the only person on the retention roster at 100%.

## Resolution Plan

Resolve in stages. Partial answers in Acts I–II (he needed them; he had been alone a very long time). The mechanical answer at the clock tower in Ch 12 — he cannot leave, and a letter is the only thing he can make leave. The emotional answer in Ch 23, when he opens the door anyway. **Do not** reveal in Act I that the letter was a rescue attempt.
