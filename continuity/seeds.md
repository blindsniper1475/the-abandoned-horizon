# Seed Registry

**What a seed is:** a catchable pattern the reader can file that the POV character does *not* name or act on (see style-sheet "Reader Ahead Of Character"). Clues are for the characters; seeds are for the reader. Never let a POV character comment on a seed aloud — that promotes it to a clue.

**Rule of thumb:** Act I = 1–3 seeds/chapter; Act II = 2–4/chapter. The reader should be able to assemble the premise by end of Act II with no character ever saying *loop*.

## Payoff ledger discipline

- Every seed needs a payoff chapter chosen by end of Act II; if a seed goes unpaid past its window, either pay it off cheaply in description or mark it `abandoned` here (never silently).
- When drafting any chapter, scan this table first: plant-or-water at least one seed per chapter (budgets above), and check whether the escalation category (style-sheet) collides with the previous chapter's.
