# The Cost Of A Reboot

## Setup

The clock tower logs establish that Alex has been re-booting the school. They also establish, quietly, that every re-boot pushes the Buffer lower and makes the distortions more visible. Alex believes he is fixing something. The logs read like the work of a man who has never once compared two entries side by side.

## Payoff

A later chapter must land the true cost: the re-boots are not failed rescues, they *are* the collapse.
- **Ch 13** — the school melts; the group realises the loops are deepening.
- **Ch 20** — the final De-Rez; the hundredth attempt is the last.
- **Ch 23** — Alex opens the door knowing it takes him with it.
