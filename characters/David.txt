# CHARACTER PROFILE: DAVID KELLER
**Hard-Coded Role:** The Analyst (Hacker)
**Current Status:** Married to Sarah Keller.
**Location:** Oregon.
**Retention:** 73% (compatibility with the Void / the Noise)

**Appearance:**
Lean and a little stooped from long hours at a desk, with restless hands and tired, sharp eyes. He dresses plainly and functionally, like a man who stopped thinking about clothes years ago.

**Personality:**
Quick-witted and intensely logical. He is the "brain" of the group, prone to over-analyzing everything, and quietly frustrated by any problem he can't take apart.

**Background:**
David and Sarah married and moved to Oregon. David worked as a systems/security analyst for a major tech firm. He remembers "learning" how to see the world as a series of systems. He remembers the school's infrastructure—the wires, the servers—more than the classes.

**Human Goal:**
To find the "Backdoor" out of the Archive.

**Reaction to De-Rez:**
He becomes obsessed with the "Buffer" percentage. He tries to "debug" the school's geometry, often becoming frustrated when the "Non-Euclidean" shifts defy his logic.
