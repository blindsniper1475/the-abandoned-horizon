# Jake Mercer

## Appearance

Six-foot-one and lanky, with the loose, unhurried slouch of a man who has not been on a schedule in years. Long, unkempt hair going silver at the edges, worn denim, a soft old jacket, three beaded bracelets on one wrist that he never takes off. He carries a battered camera the way other people carry a wallet. His eyes are the only alert thing about him.

## Personality & Traits

Easy-going and almost aggressively unhurried. Jake moves through the world like a man wading, and he is the only one of the five who does not seem afraid of the school. He is endlessly empathetic — he talks to the Echoes as though they can hear him, and grieves for them out loud. He has smoked and drifted and dropped out of every structure the others clung to, and he is, in the end, the steadiest of them.

**Strengths:** Genuinely unshockable; sees people, not situations; the only one who can look at the Horror directly
**Flaws:** Avoids responsibility by avoiding commitment; documents instead of intervening; trusts the wrong things to be harmless

## Backstory

Jake did not marry anyone and did not move anywhere in particular. After graduation he drifted west, got into weed and then into everything else, and found the one discipline that ever held him: a camera. He became a freelance photographer, good enough to live on and too unruly to be employed by anyone. His memories of high school are the sharpest of the group's — but they are all pictures. He remembers faces in frames and cannot remember a single conversation.

## Motivations & Goals

**External:** Get his people out with their story intact.
**Internal:** Stop being the observer. Get in the frame.
**Fear:** That he will photograph all of this and still not be part of it — that he will document the end of his friends and walk out with a camera full of evidence and no grief.

## Location References

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Loose, warm, unhurried. He talks in images and tangents, and he is the only one who says what he actually feels. He calls people "man" and "brother." Under pressure he does not get faster — he gets slower, and quieter, and kinder.

> "Hang on. Hang on. Look at her. She's not attacking us, man, she's knocking. She's asking to come in."

> "I've taken forty thousand pictures of other people's lives. This is the first one I'm actually in."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** The watcher — safe behind the camera, present everywhere and in nowhere.
- **Key turning points:** The Echoes that recognise him; the loop where his photographs are the only memory that survives; the moment he must choose between shooting the ending and being inside it.
- **Ending state:** Pays the price for the group's memory with his own. Jake is the one the school deletes — and the photographs he leaves behind are how the others ever find the door.
