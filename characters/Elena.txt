# CHARACTER PROFILE: ELENA HAYES
**Hard-Coded Role:** The Record Keeper (Reporter)
**Current Status:** Married to Marcus Hayes, Online Blog Reporter.
**Location:** Texas.
**Retention:** 88% (compatibility with the Void / the Noise)

**Appearance:**
Average height, with sharp, observant eyes and a practical, polished look. She keeps her dark hair pulled back and dresses for movement—functional, but never sloppy.

**Personality:**
Meticulous and intellectually curious. She notices the small details other people walk past, and she can get quietly anxious when things don't add up. She is driven by a need to know.

**Background:**
Elena and Marcus married shortly after high school and moved to Texas. She became an online blog reporter, often digging into local history and school records. Her memories of high school are "Low-Bit," but she is searching for the truth of their past in the Archive's records.

**Human Goal:**
To find the truth of what happened to them before they were "Discarded."

**Reaction to De-Rez:**
She becomes obsessed with the "Phosphor Green" glitches, trying to interpret the "Data-Rot" as a language. She is the first to notice the "Buffer" dropping.
