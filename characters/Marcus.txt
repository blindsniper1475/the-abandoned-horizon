# CHARACTER PROFILE: MARCUS HAYES
**Hard-Coded Role:** The Protector (Cop)
**Current Status:** Married to Elena Hayes, Texas Ranger.
**Location:** Texas.
**Retention:** 81% (compatibility with the Void / the Noise)

**Appearance:**
A tall, broad-shouldered man with a rugged, weathered look. He has short-cropped hair, a slight beard, and calloused hands from years of service.

**Personality:**
Stoic and steady. He speaks with a deep, calm authority. He’s the "anchor" of the group, but there's a weary weight in his eyes from years of service.

**Background:**
After high school, Marcus and Elena married and moved to Texas. Marcus joined the Texas Rangers, finding a sense of purpose in the "Line" of law and order. His memories of high school are "Low-Bit"—fading fragments of a time before the "De-Rez."

**Human Goal:**
To ensure the safety of his group. He feels a deep, subconscious guilt for being the one who "holds the line," fearing that if he fails, the others will vanish into the Void.

**Reaction to De-Rez:**
He becomes hyper-fixated on physical barriers. As the school melts, he becomes more aggressive and desperate to maintain the "Safe Zone," even if it's just a small room.
