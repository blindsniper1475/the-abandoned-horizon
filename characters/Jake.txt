# CHARACTER PROFILE: JAKE MERCER
**Hard-Coded Role:** The Witness (Tech Enthusiast)
**Current Status:** Hippie / Freelance Photographer.
**Location:** Traveling / Unknown.
**Retention:** 85% (compatibility with the Void / the Noise)

**Appearance:**
Lanky and relaxed, with long, unkempt hair and a bohemian style—worn denim, loose shirts, and a few beaded bracelets on one wrist. He moves with an easy, unhurried slouch.

**Personality:**
Easy-going and empathetic. He is the "soul" of the group, more interested in the feeling of a place than in how it works, and slow to fear the things other people call monstrous.

**Background:**
Jake embraced a hippie lifestyle, getting into weed and drugs, and became a freelance photographer. His memories of high school are tied to the images he took of his peers. He sees the "Echoes" not as monsters, but as the "Discarded" pieces of their own lives.

**Human Goal:**
To preserve the "Echoes" so they aren't forgotten.

**Reaction to De-Rez:**
He is the most empathetic to the "Echoes." While others fear the monsters, he feels a profound sadness for them, and his fear is tempered by a desire to document their "final" state.
