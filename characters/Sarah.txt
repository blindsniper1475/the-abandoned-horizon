# CHARACTER PROFILE: SARAH KELLER
**Hard-Coded Role:** The Observer (Model/Funder)
**Current Status:** Married to David Keller.
**Location:** Oregon.
**Retention:** 76% (compatibility with the Void / the Noise)

**Appearance:**
Tall and poised, with an elegant, camera-ready presence. She carries herself with a graceful stillness—always aware of where the light falls.

**Personality:**
Quietly observant and introspective. She watches more than she speaks. She feels the texture of a room—its color, its sharpness—more deeply than she lets on.

**Background:**
Sarah and David married and moved to Oregon. She worked as a model and funder, focusing on aesthetics and visual presentation. Her memories of high school are almost entirely visual—the lights, the aesthetics, the feeling of being "presented."

**Human Goal:**
To maintain her sense of self as her surroundings dissolve.

**Reaction to De-Rez:**
She is the most sensitive to the "De-Rez" effects. She experiences "Resolution Loss" as a form of sensory deprivation and becomes increasingly withdrawn.
