# Master Timeline: The Abscissa Universe

*Digital Decay, Shy Neko and Aiko in one chronology*

This timeline combines the Digital Decay documents (Story Bible, Overarching Story, Master Design Doc, Emma's dialogue), the Shy Neko lore bible, and Aiko's lore. Where the documents disagree, the entry is marked **CONFLICT** and the options are listed at the bottom. Items marked **[Proposed]** are editorial proposals, not source text.

## Era 1: Origins (before 1970)

| Date | Event | Source |
|------|-------|--------|
| Before 1900s | Pine Valley is founded on an unusually dense set of intersecting ley lines. They are thin points to the Hollow Spaces, where the Architect lives. | Overarching Story |
| 1920s | Pine Valley's industrialist families form the Abscissa Society. They want to harvest the Hollow Spaces' Cold Electricity through Tithes. | Overarching Story, Story Bible |
| 1930s | A station master sacrifices 40 passengers and becomes the Conductor. **CONFLICT** (see below). | Story Bible |
| 1962 | The Riverside Apartments are built. | Emma's dialogue |
| 1963 | The "Great Silence" on Echo Island. Abscissa builds its primary Research Spire there. | Story Bible (Case File 26) |

## Undated Material

These appear in the source documents but have no clear place in the chronology:

- **The Void, Johnny Midnight and Brother Malachi's radio broadcasts.** They describe Pine Valley under an Abscissa "dome of logic" with violet mist. They read like an event after Emma's story or a parallel layer. Decide where they belong.
- **Aiko as the "sum of the deleted files."** The continuity notes say she personifies decay but give no date beyond her 2125 origin.

## Conflicts — Resolved 2026-11-02

| Conflict | Ruling |
|----------|--------|
| Present year of Emma's story | **2006.** The Story Bible's 2008 was Yui's kidnapping year leaking across; 2008 is a different story. |
| Midnight Express | **October 31, 1979.** The Story Bible's 1982 and the Complete Timeline's 1978 are superseded. The October 31 date is fixed — it is the deliberate echo with Yui's kidnapping. |
| The Conductor's origin | Both true: a 1930s station master who was rewarded after the 1979 sacrifice. |
| End of the Dormancy | **2006**, when Emma returns. The "2020" value does not survive. |
| Emma's mother lost | **February 12, 2006.** |
| The Landlord | Arthur Vance, Riverside's superintendent. |
| Subway event year | Both true: the 1978 construction pierces the membrane, the 1979 sacrifice completes the bridge. |
| Pine Valley case dates | The Atlas's Case File Renaming Scheme rules: Midnight Express October 31, 1979; High School April 15, 1980; Mall November 28, 1980; Bridge and Hospital October 31, 1981; TV Studio March 17, 1983; Church 1983 to 1985; Arcade July 4, 1984. Era 2 therefore runs to 1985 and the Long Dormancy starts in 1986. |
| First FTL jump | A 2002 uncrewed test flight, 1,200 short uncrewed test jumps through 2007, the first sustained jump between solar systems in 2008 (uncrewed), and the first crewed transit in 2025. |
| Final selection | 24 candidates are paired into 12 duels, and 12 survive. All twelve are female. |
