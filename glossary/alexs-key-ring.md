# Alex's Key Ring

**Definition (bible only):** The Custodian's ring — every door on campus opens for something on it, including ones that should have been re-keyed twenty years ago. The title travels with the keys; the ring is why Alex can trigger a re-boot from anywhere in the building.

**On page:** Only ever *seen*: the jingle before a figure resolves, a silhouette with a round dark weight at its hip. Never described item by item, never counted by a character, never touched by anyone but Alex on page. If someone else holds it, that chapter is the hinge of the book.
