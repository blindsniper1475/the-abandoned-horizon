# Abscissa Industries

**Definition (bible only):** The corporate-occult body that built the framework (Buffer/Retention/Synthesis), administered the town's processing, and technically still owns the campus. Not a conspiracy with motives — closer to a utility company that forgot to stop billing.

**On page:** Lives on letterhead, signets, maintenance decals, and forms. First physical appearance is the foundation watermark Elena notices in Ch 3 material. Spoken aloud at most once in the whole book, flatly, when paperwork forces it. Pronunciation for audio copy: ab-SISS-uh.
