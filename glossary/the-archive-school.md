# Archive School

**Definition (bible only):** Twelve brutalist towers fused into one storm-shelter campus on the Alaskan coast; built so a whole town could live inside a White-Out without stepping outdoors. Decommissioned fifteen years ago; the world quietly stopped counting the town anyway. It is the last structure holding the Buffer up.

**On page:** In narration it is lowercase *the school* (and by wing/function: *the library*, *the greenhouse*, *the gallery*). Capitalized **Archive School** only in formal/document contexts — letterhead, directory brass, signets. Characters never explain the name; it reads to them as simply what the place was called.
