# Echoes

**Definition (bible only):** What the Noise leaves behind when it gets in. Three kinds: **Static** (frozen, repeating one action, harmless alone), **Corrupted** (aggressive distortions of discarded people), **Buffer-Breakers** (huge, non-Euclidean, erode rather than hunt).

**On page:** Never called echoes until the word arrives with evidence (Ch 14 territory). Early appearances are written as *someone standing where no one is*, a woman knocking that isn't attacking, a repetition with a rhythm. Jake talks to them like people; that is the humanizing error the book punishes later.
