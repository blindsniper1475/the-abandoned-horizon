# Loops

**Definition (bible only):** Alex's re-boots. The siege repeats roughly ten to twelve times; each loop slightly different, resets harder, until the second-to-last, when remembering earlier loops finally makes leaving possible. Carries across a loop: all digital records (Jake's photographs survive fully), key items (keys, key cards, notes), partial memory; critical injuries carry but weaken each pass, minor damage vanishes.

**On page:** No character says *loop* about their own situation until Elena can prove it (her notebook is the proof). Until then it is experienced as *already having stood here*, *the coffee still warm*, and denied: everyone reaches for a rational first explanation (bad lightbulb, timers, caretakers) — that denial is the King layer doing its job.
