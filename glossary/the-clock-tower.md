# Clock Tower

**Definition (bible only):** The campus tower where the Custodian's logs are kept — service records, sign-in ledgers, and (from Ch 12) the printed Retention artifacts. Its clock keeps correct time for the first loops and then stops keeping it consistently, which is how David first proves the resets to himself.

**On page:** *The clock tower*, lowercase generic until it becomes a destination (Ch 12, Elena POV). Time references before then stay conversational per style sheet (*four hours ago*, not timestamps).
