# Void

**Definition (bible only):** The state things fall into when reality stops holding them. Indifferent, not evil — where the world puts what it has decided to forget. The town was processed into it; the school is the last structure still outside it.

**On page:** Never named, never capitalized in narration, never given intentions. It is only ever the *absence where weather should be*, the white without detail, the pause in the radio. Lovecraft rule: seen, never spoken to.
