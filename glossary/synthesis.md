# Synthesis

**Definition (bible only):** Abscissa Industries' administrative process by which places and populations are scored, processed, and filed out of the active record. The town went through Synthesis; the school was left standing as archive infrastructure.

**On page:** Appears only in paperwork — forms, letterhead fine print, dossier headers — from Ch 16 onward. No employee voice exists in the book; the corporation is felt exclusively as stationery. Never spoken in dialogue.
