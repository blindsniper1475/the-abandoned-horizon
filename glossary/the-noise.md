# Noise

**Definition (bible only):** The Void's signal — the pressure against the Buffer. On campus it wears the shape of the White-Out: the blizzard the five hear at the walls is the Noise, not weather.

**On page:** Always *the storm*, *the wind*, *the white*. The reveal that the blizzard has no weather properties (no drifts against walls, swept courtyard, directionless moan) is planted in Ch 1–2 and pays off mid-book. Nobody names it until the tapes let them hear it separated.
