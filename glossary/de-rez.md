# De-Rez

**Definition (bible only):** The event of the Buffer failing locally — matter, light, sound, and meaning dropping resolution together. Reversible while the Buffer holds elsewhere; permanent at zero.

**On page:** Never said aloud. Written as edges softening, color going muddy, a hallway forgetting its proportions. Sarah's vocabulary carries it: *flat*, *washed out*, *unfinished*.
