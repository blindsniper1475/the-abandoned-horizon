# Rot

**Definition (bible only):** The visible symptom of a thinning Buffer — phosphor-green glitches (#00FF41), non-Euclidean geometry, texture/memory/meaning decaying together. The greenhouse is where it shows first because living tissue holds the pattern worst.

**On page:** Never named. Rendered as the flicker's wrong color, a vine cut back by no one, tables turned and turned back. Characters reach for *old wiring*, *timers*, *the foundation sending a crew*. The word *rot* itself stays out of narration until Elena writes it in her notebook underlined, well past Act I.
