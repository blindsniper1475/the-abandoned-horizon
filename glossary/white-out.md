# White-Out

**Definition (bible only):** Nominally the coastal blizzard class the school was built to shelter a whole town through. Actually the visible face of the Noise pressing the campus. The town's designated refuge protocol — everyone indoors, kitchen running all night — is why the building reads as *ready* even now.

**On page:** Fully allowed, used constantly, always hyphenated as a noun (*the White-Out*) and lowercased as a condition (*white-out conditions*). Weather talk about it is the sanctioned way characters discuss the horror without knowing.
