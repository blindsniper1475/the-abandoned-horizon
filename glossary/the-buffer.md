# Buffer

**Definition (bible only):** The skin of coherence that holds a place in reality. In Act I it is at near-100% and failing; it drops to 0% across the twenty-four chapters. Alex's maintenance work is, mechanically, Buffer upkeep.

**On page:** Never named until late Act II at the earliest, and then only from paperwork (a log line, a form field), never in speech. Characters experience it as *the room holding* or *not holding*. Style sheet Terms rule applies: sensation only.
