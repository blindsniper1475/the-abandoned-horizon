# Retention

**Definition (bible only):** A person's scored compatibility with the Void/Noise — how long they hold when the world stops holding them. Alex scored 100%; the five sit between 73% and 88%. This score is why sixty letters brought exactly five people: the others could not have survived the drive back to the town.

**On page:** Numbers appear only as printed artifacts (clock-tower logs, Abscissa dossiers, Ch 12+). No character ever says their own number aloud before Act III, and percentages are never narrated. The reader learns the shape of the idea through Marcus's dossier (his name under a number) before the word exists for it.
