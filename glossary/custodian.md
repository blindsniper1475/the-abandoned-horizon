# Custodian

**Definition (bible only):** The job title for whoever keeps the Archive School running — facilities, logs, and (unknowingly, then knowingly) the Buffer. Alex Voss is the last and only current holder. The title travels with the keys.

**On page:** Allowed — it is an official title, and it appears first in documents (signet, ledger signature block, service door placards) before any living person uses it. Capitalized only in document contexts; lowercase *the custodian* in narration after Ch 6 establishes who he is.
