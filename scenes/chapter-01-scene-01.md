# The Arrival

## Purpose

Establish the five, the reunion, and the pristine-but-too-sharp school. Plant the first wrongness (the flicker), the fact that almost nobody came, and the sense that the place is *too* clean. End on the reveal that the school is not empty.

## Continuity Notes

- All five are present, healthy, and unaware anything is wrong.
- Elena begins the written log that will become the group's memory across loops.
- Alex is present but unseen — **mentioned only**, never in the scene cast.
- The storm is established as a cage, not weather.
- Marcus counts heads: a habit that pays off every time the loops reset.
