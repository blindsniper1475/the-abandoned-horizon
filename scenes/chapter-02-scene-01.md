# The Sanctuary

## Purpose

The group leaves the common room after the flicker and crosses the school to find beds. Elena maps as they walk; the route takes them through the library (a hand-kept ledger dated eleven days ago) and the vertical greenhouse (vines cut back this week, power nobody pays). Her map fails to match the geography she walked. They find exactly five rooms made up in a gallery of fifty, one of them left lit and empty. The chapter's question: the letter went to sixty — how did the building know it would be five?

## Continuity Notes

- All five are healthy; the building is still pristine and warm.
- Elena logs physical symptoms (eye ache, chest warmth) after the greenhouse, attributes them to travel, tells no one — first bodily step of the duress curve, never named.
- The circulation ledger: entries in an unknown hand, dated eleven days before arrival; Elena copies four lines (names/times) into her notebook — seeds the Ch 3 archive discovery.
- Room assignments: Marcus+Elena in 103, David 105, Sarah 109, Jake at the end before the stair; 107 made up and left empty with its lamp burning.
