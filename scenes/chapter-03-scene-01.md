# The Archive Vaults

## Purpose

First Retention sighting (plot point 3). The school stops being hospitality and starts being paperwork: someone has been *filing* the five since graduation. Dread arrives as bureaucracy — current addresses, current spouses, current jobs — not as a monster. Sarah's POV earns its rotation slot by being the one who notices what the documents are made of: the photographs, and the difference between a photograph and a record. Hook lands as revelation in the final third: Alex's file is last, dated every year, still open, 100%.

## Beat Map

1. **Descent** — morning after Ch 2; storm still holding; the group follows service stairs down from the library into the archive vaults (lowered ceilings, flat light, index drawers). Marcus counts doors out loud; Jake photographs everything because the light down here is even.
2. **The cabinets** — class records by year; they find their own graduation-year boxes. Warmth first: names, pranks, teachers they all remember wrong the same way. Then the second drawer: *post-graduation* folders, current as of days ago.
3. **Reading order (locked):** Jonas Fells (the J-name classmate; highest number; never came) → Marcus → David → Elena → Sarah (second to last) → Alex Voss (last). Each dossier carries: relationship status, current address, occupation, notable events, family ties, and a percentage. Nobody reacts big; the room cools by degrees.
4. **Turn/decision** — Elena writes the numbers down without comment; David asks what changed; Sarah wants to know why the photographs look *shot*, professionally, of people who were never famous. Marcus says they take the folders upstairs. End contract met: they move, carrying the files.
