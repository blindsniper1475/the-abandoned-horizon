# The First Echo

## Purpose

David's method — measure, explain, fix — breaks for the first time: his own measurements contradict each other, and the group stops trusting the building's hospitality. The first Echo appears (never named): a motionless figure in the white-out that moves only when unobserved, then a metronome knock at the walkway door that cuts off mid-rhythm. Jake begins humanizing the phenomena ("a person keeping time") — the error the book punishes in Ch 14. Ends with the five sleeping together in the common room; safety-by-separation is over.
