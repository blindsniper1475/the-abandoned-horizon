# Digital Decay

## Overview

The physics of the story. Reality in the Archive is **held together by a Buffer** — a skin of coherence — and that skin can be worn thin. When it fails, the world does not break; it *loses resolution*. Edges soften, colours go muddy, sound thins out, and the space stops obeying the shape of space. This is **De-Rez**.

The friends never say any of these words out loud. In the prose it is described as a place going *flat*, *wrong*, *washed out*, or *unfinished*.

## Rules & Limitations

**THE VOID** — the state things fall into when reality stops holding them. Not evil; indifferent. Where the world puts what it has decided to forget.

**THE ROT** — the visible symptom. Phosphor-green glitches (#00FF41), non-Euclidean geometry, and "data-rot": texture, memory, and meaning decaying together.

**THE NOISE** — the Void's signal. What the friends hear as the White-Out blizzard is the Noise pressing against the school.

**THE BUFFER** — the barrier that holds a place together. Drops from 100% to 0% over the twenty-four episodes. Never spoken as a number on the page.

**DE-REZ** — the Buffer failing. The world melts into a liminal, low-resolution state.

**RETENTION** — every person is scored on compatibility with the Void / the Noise. The higher the number, the longer they hold. Alex scored **100%**. The five sit between **73% and 88%**.

**THE ECHOES** — what the Noise leaves behind when it gets in. Three kinds: **Static Echoes** (frozen, repeating a single action, harmless alone); **Corrupted Echoes** (aggressive distortions of discarded people); **Buffer-Breakers** (huge, non-Euclidean, erode rather than hunt).

**THE LOOPS** — the siege repeats roughly **ten to twelve times**. Each loop is a re-boot, each slightly different. Resets get harder until the second-to-last, when remembering the earlier loops finally makes it easier.

**What carries across a loop:** anything digital survives fully (files, records, and Jake's photographs); key items come through (key cards, keys, photographs, notes); **memories carry only partially**. **Critical injuries carry over but are less severe each loop; minor damage disappears completely.**

**Duress** — the body fails on the same curve as the world: **the Chill** (flu-like), **the Drift** (disorientation), **the Fracture** (fragmented memory), **the Forgetting** (losing oneself).

## History

Abscissa Industries built the framework. The town was processed, scored, and eventually discarded — filed rather than destroyed. The school was the last structure holding the Buffer up, and its Custodian was the last person holding the school.

## Practitioners

- **Alex Voss** — the Custodian. The only person who can trigger a re-boot.
- **Abscissa Industries** — the corporate-occult body that administers Synthesis and Archives.
- **The five** — subjects, scored, and never told.

## Impact on Society

There is no society left to impact. That is the point: the decay is not a disaster happening *to* a town, it is the town being quietly taken out of the record while a handful of people stand in it and insist it is real.
