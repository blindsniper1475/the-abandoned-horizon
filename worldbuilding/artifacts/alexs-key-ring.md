# Alex's Key Ring

## Description

A steel ring heavy with keys — brass mortice keys, older flat keys ground thin by use, and a fan of plastic key cards of the kind that open doors in buildings still connected to something. Alex never sets it down. It is the only object in the school that is unambiguously his.

## Function

The ring opens what the Custodian manages: the sub-basement, the deep files, the clock tower, and the maintenance spine that links all twelve towers. In story terms it is an **access token** — the physical permission to move through parts of the school the ordinary corridors do not reach.

It is also one of the **key items that carries across a loop**, so it survives a reset and can be found again.

## History

Issued to the Custodian when the school was commissioned. Every key was cut for a door that mattered once, in a town that has since been taken out of the record.

## Current State

On Alex's person, in the maintenance coat, in the long straight hallways. Not yet seen by any of the five. It first reaches them as a sound — keys somewhere ahead, that stop when they stop.
