# Abscissa Industries

## Purpose

A corporate-occult conglomerate that treats human consciousness as **data to be optimised**. Abscissa does not destroy places it no longer wants. It **discards** them — files them away into an Archive, where they continue to exist at reduced resolution, still inhabited, still running. Nobody signs a form for this. The paperwork is the discarding.

## Power Base

- **Synthesis** — the creation of protected, "pristine" realities. Abscissa's product.
- **Archives** — the storage of realities that have been discarded. Abscissa's landfill.
- **Retention scoring** — Abscissa's method of sorting people. A subject's percentage determines how long they can hold inside a decaying Archive before the Void takes them.

Abscissa's real leverage is not money. It is that it decides what stays in the record.
